# Music

Kefyros plays MP3 files from `/kefyros/music`, including subfolders. The player
indexes up to 1,500 tracks and reads ID3 title, artist, album, track number,
duration and embedded JPEG or PNG cover art. If a track has no embedded cover,
it also checks for `Folder.jpg`, `cover.jpg`, `front.jpg` or `Folder.png` in the
same folder.

## Browse and play

- **Tab** or **m** cycles through All Songs, By Artist, By Album and Files.
- **Up / Down** moves the selection; **Enter** opens an artist, album or folder,
  or plays the selected MP3. Enter or **Space** pauses or resumes the current track.
- **Backspace** leaves an artist or album, or moves to the parent folder in Files.
- **Left / Right** seeks five seconds; **[ / ]** skips to the previous or next track.
- **s** toggles shuffle. **r** cycles repeat off, all and one.
- **b** switches between the speaker and a connected Bluetooth audio device.
- **F5** rebuilds the music index after adding files.
- **F1** turns off the screen and locks the controls; press F1 again to unlock.
- **Esc** leaves Music and stops playback.

The lower display shows the track, playback time, shuffle/repeat state and
left/right audio levels. Bluetooth headphones are connected in the Bluetooth app.
