# First Launch and SD Card

1. Use a microSD card formatted as **FAT32 or exFAT**.
2. Extract `kefyros_sd.zip` at the card's root. The resulting path should be
   `/kefyros/icons/calc.png`, not a ZIP-named folder around `kefyros`.
3. Insert the card and power on Kefyros.

If the card is missing or unreadable, Kefyros shows an SD CARD warning. If the
card is empty or missing supplied files, extract the service pack again. The
warning names the first missing file. Sound effects are optional.

When updating a card, keep `/kefyros/config.txt` and your music, notes, chats,
ROMs, game files and saves. The service pack only supplies system assets and
empty folders; it does not need a card format to be reapplied.
